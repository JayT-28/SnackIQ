# SnackIQ

**Know what you're really eating in seconds**

Clear, opinionated verdicts for teens about the snacks we eat. No nutrition degree required.

---

## Motivation

Nutrition labels are confusing, misleading, and time-consuming to interpret. Marketing claims like "all natural" or "lightly sweetened" tell us nothing about what we're actually putting in our body.

**SnackIQ exists to give you straight answers:**

- **By teens, for teens.**
- **Owned by students.**
- **Features driven by us.**

The goal is simple: Help teens like us make informed snack choices in seconds, not minutes. You shouldn't need a nutrition degree to know if something is good for us.

- **Opinionated** - Clear verdicts, not vague health claims
- **Real Data** - Powered by Open Food Facts community database
- **Ingredient Alerts** - Highlights concerning additives and preservatives
